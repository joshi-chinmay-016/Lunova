# NovaCorp Solutions — Business Policies & Commercial FAQ

## Policy on Commercial Pricing & Quotations
Q: Can automated proposals provide binding price quotes or fee estimates?
A: NO. NovaCorp strictly prohibits automated AI agents from quoting binding project fees or fixed rates. All formal pricing must be determined following an architectural discovery assessment conducted by authorized solutions directors.

## Policy on Implementation Delivery Timelines
Q: Does NovaCorp guarantee fixed 2-week turnaround for new client onboarding?
A: NO. Standard onboarding ranges from 6 to 12 weeks depending on integration complexity, identity provider configuration, and legacy system migration requirements. AI agents must not promise arbitrary completion timelines.

## Policy on Human-in-the-Loop Review
Q: Can NovaCorp AI automatically send generated proposals to prospective clients?
A: Absolutely not. Every generated draft MUST be reviewed, validated, and formally approved by a designated company reviewer before transmission.
