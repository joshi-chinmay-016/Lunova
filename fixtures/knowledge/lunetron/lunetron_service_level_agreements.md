# Lunetron — Service Level Agreements (SLAs) & Support Terms

## Operational Uptime Commitment
Lunetron guarantees monthly service availability according to client subscription tier:
- **Tier 1 (Enterprise Mission Critical)**: 99.99% monthly uptime availability guarantee.
- **Tier 2 (Business Essential)**: 99.9% monthly uptime availability guarantee.
- **Tier 3 (Standard / Developer)**: 99.5% monthly uptime availability guarantee.

## Incident Severity Definitions & Response Times
| Severity Level | Definition | Tier 1 Response Time | Tier 2 Response Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Severity 1 (Critical)** | Core platform unavailable; production email sync or proposal delivery down; critical data integrity risk. | < 15 minutes (24/7/365) | < 1 hour (24/7) |
| **Severity 2 (Major)** | Major platform feature impaired; workarounds exist; non-critical API degradation. | < 1 hour (24/7/365) | < 4 hours (Business Hours) |
| **Severity 3 (Minor)** | Minor cosmetic or functional issue with negligible business impact. | < 4 hours | < 1 business day |
| **Severity 4 (General)** | Documentation queries, feature requests, administrative configuration questions. | < 1 business day | < 2 business days |

## Service Credits & Remedies
If Lunetron fails to meet the guaranteed monthly uptime, customers are eligible for service credits applied to their subsequent billing invoice:
- Monthly Uptime 99.0% - 99.89%: 10% credit of monthly platform fee.
- Monthly Uptime 95.0% - 98.99%: 25% credit of monthly platform fee.
- Monthly Uptime < 95.0%: 50% credit of monthly platform fee.

## Support Coverage & Dedicated Personnel
Tier 1 Enterprise clients are assigned a dedicated Named Technical Account Manager (TAM), a Principal Solutions Architect, and direct escalation access to the engineering response team via Slack/Teams Connect.
