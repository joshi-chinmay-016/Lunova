# Lunetron — Standard Enterprise Pricing Models & Engagements

## Platform Licensing Tiers
Lunetron licenses its enterprise platform infrastructure under annual recurring software subscription agreements:

### 1. Enterprise Core Tier
- **Target Customer**: Mid-market enterprises initiating automated proposal response operations.
- **Price**: $12,000 per month (billed annually at $144,000/year).
- **Inclusions**: Up to 50 active user seats; up to 1,000 automated proposals processed per month; standard multi-tenant cloud hosting; Tier 2 Business Essential SLA.

### 2. Enterprise Scale Tier
- **Target Customer**: Global enterprises with high-volume RFP workflows and complex multi-region compliance needs.
- **Price**: $28,000 per month (billed annually at $336,000/year).
- **Inclusions**: Unlimited user seats; up to 10,000 automated proposals processed per month; dedicated isolated virtual private cloud (VPC) deployment; Tier 1 Mission Critical SLA (99.99% uptime); custom LLM fine-tuning pipelines.

## Implementation & Professional Services Packages
- **Architecture Discovery Sprint**: $45,000 fixed fee (4 weeks). Includes end-to-end security audit, data ingestion schema mapping, and infrastructure sizing.
- **Production Deployment & Pilot**: $65,000 fixed fee (6 weeks). Includes full knowledge base ingestion, email connector integration, custom prompt guardrail calibration, and user training.
- **Ongoing Architecture Retainer**: $35,000 per month. Dedicated senior engineering advisory, quarterly performance tuning, and compliance audit support.

## Commercial Policies & Guardrails
- **Payment Terms**: Net 30 days from invoice date.
- **Travel & Expenses**: On-site workshops and architecture sessions billed at cost with advance client written approval.
- **Discounts**: Volume discounts apply to multi-year contracts (10% discount for 2-year commitments; 15% discount for 3-year commitments).
- **Custom Scopes**: Any custom functional scope, proprietary database adapters, or legacy on-premises deployment requires a formal, executed Statement of Work (SOW).
