- **Solution**: Lunetron architected a resilient, distributed event-driven settlement backbone on AWS EKS and deployed an automated, citation-grounded proposal response system across 4 regional desks.

## Case Study 2: Leading Healthcare HealthTech Network
- **Client**: National Health Information Exchange Network (50M+ Patient Records)
- **Challenge**: Critical need to modernize unstructured clinical document ingestion while guaranteeing HIPAA compliance, SOC 2 Type II controls, and absolute isolation between regional hospital systems.
- **Solution**: Lunetron engineered an automated document processing pipeline with structural OCR and semantic chunking, storing 768-dimensional vector representations in hardened multi-tenant PostgreSQL clusters.
- **Results**:
  - Over 50,000 clinical inquiry documents processed daily with sub-second retrieval latency.
  - 99.8% citation accuracy with verifiable provenance for clinical audit teams.
  - Successfully defended third-party SOC 2 Type II audit with zero findings.
