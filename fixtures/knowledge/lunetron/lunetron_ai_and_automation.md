# Lunetron — AI Systems & Intelligent Workflow Automation

## Sovereign AI & Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Lunetron designs and implements enterprise-grade RAG systems that ground all generative AI outputs in verified tenant knowledge:
- **Zero Hallucination Tolerance**: Every claim generated by Lunetron AI engines must be accompanied by a verifiable citation pointing to authoritative indexed company collateral.
- **Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Embeddings and retrieved chunks are strictly filtered at query time using cryptographic and tenant-scoped `company_id` constraints in `pgvector`.
- **Hybrid Search Pipelines**: Combining dense semantic vector retrieval (using 768-dimensional text embeddings) with BM25 keyword matching and cross-encoder re-ranking.

## Document Ingestion & Attachment Extraction
- **Multi-Format Parsing**: Automated parsing and structural text extraction from PDF, DOCX, TXT, and scanned image documents.
- **Semantic & Structural Chunking**: Chunking algorithms preserve section titles, headers, bullet lists, and tabular data up to 400-word spans with 80-word contextual overlaps.
- **Deterministic Preprocessing**: Elimination of duplicate whitespace, normalizing Unicode, and stripping malicious payload artifacts before vectorization.

## Large Language Model Orchestration
- **Supported LLM Gateways**: Unified, vendor-agnostic abstraction layer connecting to Google Gemini (Pro, Flash), Anthropic Claude, and OpenAI GPT-4o.
- **Deterministic Grounding Evaluation**: Automated post-generation verification scoring output coverage, factual consistency, and ungrounded ratio. Drafts failing strict confidence thresholds are flagged for human remediation.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Governance**: Automated generation produces drafts only. No proposal or correspondence may be dispatched externally without explicit, role-authorized reviewer approval.

## Automated Proposal Response Platform (Lunova)
Lunetron's flagship proposal automation engine automates the entire RFP response cycle:
1. Ingestion of client inquiries and RFPs via Gmail API integration.
2. In-depth requirement extraction and classification (e.g. security, pricing, SLA, technical scope).
3. Precision context retrieval from Lunetron's verified enterprise knowledge base.
4. Grounded draft proposal generation with inline source citations.
5. Interactive reviewer approval workflow with audit logging.
6. Automated outbound transmission via authenticated enterprise email channels.
