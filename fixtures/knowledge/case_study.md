# NovaCorp Solutions — Enterprise Case Study: Project Beacon Automation

## Client Profile
Fictional Global Logistics Partner (Apex Logistics Corp).

## The Challenge
Apex Logistics processed over 400 inbound RFP and freight bidding emails per week across multiple European freight hubs. Response turnaround was sluggish (averaging 72 hours), leading to missed procurement bidding windows.

## The NovaCorp Solution
NovaCorp deployed the Atlas Agentic Workflow Engine coupled with pgvector-based RAG knowledge retrieval:
- Normalized inbound bidding requests from connected email inboxes.
- Parsed PDF and Word RFPs to extract mandatory route constraints, volume requirements, and hazardous material specifications.
- Retrieved pre-approved compliance guarantees and fleet telemetry from Apex's sovereign knowledge base.
- Generated grounded draft responses citing verified fleet capabilities within 30 seconds for dispatcher review.

## Quantifiable Results
- 84% reduction in proposal draft generation turnaround time (from 72 hours to under 12 minutes including human review).
- Zero hallucinated route commitments or fabricated rate cards across 12,000 processed bids.
- 100% tenant data separation verified through independent security penetration audits.
